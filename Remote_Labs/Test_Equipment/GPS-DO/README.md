# ORI Remote Lab West GPS-DO

In 2008-2009, a group of people associated with TAPR arranged for a group buy of surplus Andrew/Grayson assemblies based on a [Trimble Thunderbolt](http://www.leapsecond.com/pages/tapr-tbolt/) GPS Disciplined Clock, originally intended for wireless infrastructure. I (KB5MU) did not participate in the group buy, but I obtained one of the units later, along with the power supply group buyers had rounded up.

I built up a monitor system based on a [Netduino Plus 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netduino), this [interface shield](https://m1dst-co-uk.stacktemp.com/shop/thunderbolt-monitor-kit/), and this [Trimble Thunderbolt Monitor software by M1DST](https://github.com/m1dst/Trimble-Thunderbolt-Monitor) (manual here in [docx](Trimble_Monitor_Instructions_v1.1.docx) or [pdf](Trimble_Monitor_Instructions_v1.1.pdf)).

I added a [TAPR TADD-1 RF Distribution Amplifier Kit](https://tapr.org/product/tadd-1-rf-distribution-amplifier/) with six 10MHz outputs for various test equipment in the Remote Lab ([TADD-1 Manual](TADD-1_Manual.pdf)) and a nice exterior antenna.
